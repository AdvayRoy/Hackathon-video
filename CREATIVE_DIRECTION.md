# NEURASCOPE — Launch film, creative direction

**Thesis.** The interface is the hero. The film is a single, calm laboratory session: one published reel goes in, the synthetic audience runs, attention fractures, the analyst explains, a patch is re-simulated. No narrator; the product's own language does the talking. Every on-screen sentence is either a claim the brief makes or a label that appears in the UI.

**Form.** 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 78 s · hard cuts on the beat, two dips to ink. Real screen recording at native 2× scale, reframed with slow GSAP camera moves (≤1.9×). Typography: Geist Sans (statements) + Geist Mono (instrument labels) — the product's own type. Palette: product palette only (ink #0b0c0e, fg #eceae4, blue #8fb3ff signal, orange #ff7a45 fracture, mint #9be3c7 counterfactual). No neon, no glow, no stock imagery.

**Structure (film time).**
0–5 cold open (two statements) · 5–8 the published reel · 8–14 input: link, audience, run · 14–18 pipeline stages, "10,000 seeded synthetic viewers" · 18–26 workspace reveal, "CORTEX predicts where attention breaks / ten times a second" · 26–33 instrument punch-ins (timeline fractures, reviewer cohorts) · 33–41 Fracture F1 inspector "Where. When. Why." · 41–50 Ask NEURASCOPE (typing → deterministic analyst) · 50–58 Pre-flight Brief → SIMULATE PATCH → +3.4 pts · 58–62 Oriane corpus evidence · 62–67 closing wide with counterfactual trace · 67–72 boundary card · 72–78 wordmark + product statement + honesty footnote.

**Sound.** No source audio exists. Original synthesized score (deterministic, `scripts/score.mjs`): sub drone + slow additive pad in D minor, 96 BPM pulse arriving with the workspace, lift on the counterfactual, resolve on the wordmark. Sparse SFX (soft clicks on cuts, ping on +3.4 pts, low impact on the wordmark).

**Reality rules applied.** All numbers shown are the ones in the recording (67 / 41 % / 0.28 / 40 %, F1 12.90–13.50 s, F1 18.2–19.1 s, +3.4 pts, 22 % → 24 %, 6 relevant published videos). Copy uses brief language verbatim where it matters: "10,000 seeded synthetic viewers", "Oriane observes · CORTEX predicts · platform analytics validate later", "Test on synthetic attention before you spend real attention", "Synthetic viewers are not people", "not a guaranteed uplift". Nothing is recreated as fake UI; the wordmark end card mirrors the landing screen's real typography.
